# Image sources — Star Electric Enterprises

Record of every photograph used on the site: what it shows, where it came
from, and the licence it is used under. Generated during the imagery pass;
keep it updated whenever an image is added or replaced.

---

## 1. Sourcing policy actually applied

The brief asked for manufacturer product photography first. That route was
**not** used, for two reasons, and you should know both:

1. **Access.** Manufacturer sites blocked automated access during research
   (Schneider Electric returned HTTP 403, as did the CDN tested).
2. **Licensing — the important one.** Manufacturer and retailer product
   photography is copyrighted. Copying it onto a commercial store without a
   distributor or brand agreement carries real legal risk, and it would also
   imply Star Electric Enterprises stocks those brands, which has not been
   established.

So every photograph below comes from **Wikimedia Commons** under a Creative
Commons or public-domain licence that permits commercial reuse. These are
genuine photographs of real electrical hardware — not stock lifestyle shots,
not AI renders, not illustrations.

Explicitly **not** used: Shutterstock, Adobe Stock, iStock, Freepik, Unsplash,
Pexels, AI-generated imagery, or scraped retailer photography.

---

## 2. ⚠ Attribution requirement — action needed before production

Most images below are **CC BY** or **CC BY-SA**. Both require visible
attribution to the photographer. CC BY-SA additionally requires that
*modified versions of the image itself* be shared under the same licence
(images here were resized/re-encoded, which counts as modification).

**Before this site goes live you must either:**

- add a credits page listing the photographer, licence and source for each
  image (the table below has everything needed), and link it from the footer; **or**
- replace the CC BY-SA images with manufacturer-supplied photography once
  Star Electric Enterprises has supplier agreements in place.

Public-domain images carry no attribution obligation.

**Permission/licensing should be confirmed before production use** for every
CC BY-SA entry, since share-alike obligations depend on how the images are
finally deployed.

## 3. Processing applied to every image

| Step | Detail |
|---|---|
| Download | Original from `upload.wikimedia.org` at 1920px |
| Product images | 1200x900 (4:3), photo contained on white with 6% padding |
| Category images | 900x560 (16:10), cover crop |
| Banners | 1800x760 (hero) / 1200x620 (promos), cover crop |
| Format | WebP, quality 0.86 |
| Markup | explicit `width`/`height`, `loading="lazy"` below the fold, descriptive `alt` |

No image was cropped in a way that misrepresents the product. No watermarks,
competitor branding, embedded prices or promotional text are present.
