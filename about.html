About Star Electric Enterprises

An electrical products store based in Saddar, Rawalpindi, supplying both everyday customers and the trade.

Who we are

Star Electric Enterprises is an electrical products store located in Saddar, Rawalpindi. We supply the materials used in everyday electrical work — wiring and cable, switches and sockets, circuit protection, lighting, fans, power equipment, smart devices and the tools that go with them.

Our customers range from homeowners buying a single replacement fitting to electricians and contractors ordering materials for a full installation. The store and this website are set up to serve both.

Our location

Saddar is one of Rawalpindi’s established commercial areas, and our physical store means customers can see products before buying, collect an order the same day, or talk through a specification in person.

Exact shop address, phone number, WhatsApp number and business hours are still to be supplied and appear as placeholders throughout the site.

What we supply

The catalogue is organised into nine departments so that customers can find a part by what it does rather than by guessing at a product name:

  • Wiring & Accessories — cable, cord, glands, conduit and connectors
  • Circuit Protection & Distribution — breakers, fuses, boards, relays and changeover gear
  • Switches & Sockets — modular plates, power sockets, industrial outlets and dimmers
  • Lighting & Fixtures — bulbs, panels, downlights and outdoor fittings
  • Fans & Ventilation — ceiling, bracket and exhaust fans
  • Power & Energy — stabilisers, meters and backup equipment
  • Smart Home Devices — app-controlled switches, plugs and sensors
  • Tools & Accessories — testers, hand tools and power tools
  • Electrical Accessories — tape, extension boards, junction boxes and consumables

How we work with customers

Buying electrical materials usually comes down to getting the rating, size and specification right. We would rather spend a few minutes confirming that with a customer than have the wrong part go out of the door.

For larger requirements we quote in writing. Contractors, electricians and builders can send an item list or bill of quantities and receive a quotation covering the materials for a job, rather than pricing line by line.

Retail and trade

Retail customers get a straightforward online shop with clear pricing and stock status. Professional buyers get the same catalogue plus a quotation route for project quantities, and can order the same materials repeatedly without re-explaining a specification.
